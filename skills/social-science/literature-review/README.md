# literature-review: Literature review and synthesis matrix

Turns a research question and your papers into a screening log, a literature matrix, a thematic synthesis, the research gap and a draft literature review.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A research question plus PDFs, reading notes or a reading list, or permission to search |
| **You get back** | Screening log with inclusion and exclusion criteria, literature matrix, synthesis organised by debate, research gap and a draft literature review section with traceable citations |
| **Method** | PRISMA-style screening, synthesis matrix, thematic synthesis |
| **Search terms** | literature review, how to write a literature review, synthesis matrix, literature matrix, research gap |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

# abstract-writer: Abstract writer for papers and theses

Turns a paper draft or thesis into abstracts at the word limits you need, plus keywords and a plain-language summary.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Paper draft, thesis or its sections, the word limit and the journal or university guidelines |
| **You get back** | Structured and unstructured abstract versions with word counts, move map, keywords, one-sentence plain-language summary, gaps |
| **Method** | Move structure of research abstracts (background, aim, method, results, conclusion) |
| **Search terms** | write an abstract, abstract generator, structured abstract, thesis abstract, journal abstract word limit |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

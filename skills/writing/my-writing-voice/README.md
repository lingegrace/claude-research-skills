# my-writing-voice: Write in your own voice

Turns samples of your own writing into a reusable voice guide, then drafts or rewrites text that sounds like you.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Your past emails, posts, essays or articles, or brand-voice samples your team owns, plus the text you need written |
| **You get back** | A saved voice guide (features, do and don't rules, examples), a draft in that voice, and a match check |
| **Method** | Stylometric features written up as a style guide |
| **Search terms** | write in my voice, writing style guide from samples, brand voice guide, match my writing style, personal writing voice |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

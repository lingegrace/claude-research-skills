# thesis-chapter-review: Thesis and dissertation chapter feedback

Turns a thesis or dissertation chapter into a rubric-scored review with strengths, the top 5 fixes and paragraph-level comments.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A thesis or dissertation chapter draft, and your marking criteria or rubric if you have it |
| **You get back** | Scores per criterion with evidence, strengths, top 5 priority fixes, paragraph-level comments, next-draft plan |
| **Method** | Rubric-based assessment; feed up, feed back, feed forward (Hattie and Timperley) |
| **Search terms** | dissertation feedback, thesis chapter review, marking rubric feedback, review my literature review chapter, dissertation proofreading and feedback |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

# human-writing: Edit an AI draft toward your voice

Edits an AI draft sentence by sentence toward the way you write, keeps every fact, and explains each change.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | An AI draft (email, essay, post, report or bio), plus one to three pieces you wrote yourself |
| **You get back** | A rewrite closer to your voice, a change log with the reason for each edit, and claims flagged as needing a source |
| **Method** | Voice-first editing: your own writing is the baseline; common AI patterns are signals, judged case by case |
| **Search terms** | humanize AI text, make AI writing sound human, rewrite ChatGPT text, remove AI tone, signs of AI writing |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

# human-writing: Make AI text sound human

Turns an AI-sounding or stiff draft into a natural rewrite that keeps every fact, with a list of what changed and why.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A draft email, essay, post, report or bio that sounds generic or machine-written |
| **You get back** | A rewrite that sounds like a person, a change log with the pattern behind each edit, and claims flagged as needing a source |
| **Method** | Reader-first editing, patterns from Wikipedia's 'Signs of AI writing' guide |
| **Search terms** | humanize AI text, make AI writing sound human, rewrite ChatGPT text, remove AI tone, signs of AI writing |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

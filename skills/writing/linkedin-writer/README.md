# linkedin-writer: Write LinkedIn posts from notes

Turns an idea, notes, an article or a research brief into three LinkedIn post options with hooks, soft CTAs and first comments.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | An idea, meeting or reading notes, an article, a research brief, and your goal (reach, authority, leads) |
| **You get back** | Three post options with different hooks, a suggested first comment for each, a fact check, and questions for real stats or stories |
| **Method** | Hook, story or insight, takeaway structure; one idea per post; LinkedIn formatting norms |
| **Search terms** | LinkedIn post writer, LinkedIn hook ideas, write a LinkedIn post, LinkedIn thought leadership post, turn article into LinkedIn post |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

# ethics-application: Research ethics application and consent form drafts

Turns a study plan into draft ethics application answers, a participant information sheet and a consent form.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Your study plan or proposal, and your institution's ethics form or guidance if you have it |
| **You get back** | Draft answers for each ethics section, risk and mitigation table, participant information sheet, consent form, open issues |
| **Method** | Belmont Report principles (respect, beneficence, justice) and standard informed consent elements |
| **Search terms** | ethics application example, IRB application, participant information sheet template, informed consent form, research ethics approval dissertation |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

# peer-review-response: Response to reviewers letter writer

Turns reviewer and editor comments into a polite point-by-point response letter and a revision checklist.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Reviewer reports, editor's decision letter, examiner comments, your manuscript or revised draft |
| **You get back** | Point-by-point response letter, cover note to the editor, list of points needing your decision, revision checklist |
| **Method** | Standard response-to-reviewers structure: comment, response, change made with location |
| **Search terms** | response to reviewers, reply to reviewer comments, rebuttal letter, major revision response, point-by-point response letter |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

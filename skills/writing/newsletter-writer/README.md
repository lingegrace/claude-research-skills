# newsletter-writer: Write a newsletter issue from research

Turns a research brief, notes or links into a full newsletter issue with subject lines, evidence-backed sections and a teaser post.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A research brief, notes or links, your audience, and past issues for voice |
| **You get back** | Subject line options, a complete issue (opening, sections, takeaway, sign-off), a teaser post and a source log |
| **Method** | One thesis per issue, evidence-backed sections, 'so what?' reader-value check |
| **Search terms** | newsletter writer, write a newsletter issue, email newsletter template, Substack newsletter draft, newsletter subject lines |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

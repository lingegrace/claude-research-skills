# interview-to-article: Turn interview transcripts into articles

Turns an interview transcript into a feature article or Q&A with verbatim quotes, headline options and a quote-approval list.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | An interview transcript (expert, customer, founder) and what the article is for |
| **You get back** | A feature article or Q&A, headline options, a quote-approval list with transcript locations, and open questions for the interviewee |
| **Method** | Journalistic feature structure (lede, nut graf, body, kicker), verbatim quoting |
| **Search terms** | interview transcript to article, write a feature article, customer story from interview, Q&A article format, expert interview write-up |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

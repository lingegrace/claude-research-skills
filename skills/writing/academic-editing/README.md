# academic-editing: Academic English editing for theses and papers

Turns rough thesis, paper or essay paragraphs into clear academic English with a categorised change log and comments on argument flow.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Thesis chapter, paper draft or essay paragraphs, plus the target journal or degree level and citation style |
| **You get back** | Edited text, change log by category (clarity, concision, hedging, signposting, grammar), recurring error patterns, argument flow comments |
| **Method** | Academic register conventions: Hyland's hedging and metadiscourse, topic sentences, signposting, given-new cohesion |
| **Search terms** | academic proofreading, thesis editing, academic English for non-native speakers, edit my essay, paper language polishing |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

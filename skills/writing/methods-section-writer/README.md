# methods-section-writer: Methods section and methodology chapter writer

Turns study design notes into a methods section draft and a checklist of reporting items you still need to add.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Notes on participants, sampling, instruments, procedure, analysis and ethics; your target journal or degree |
| **You get back** | Methods section draft by subsection, the reporting guideline that applies, a reporting checklist with missing items |
| **Method** | Reporting guidelines: COREQ, SRQR, STROBE, CONSORT, GRAMMS, APA JARS, PRISMA |
| **Search terms** | write methods section, methodology chapter, research methods writing, COREQ checklist, how to write methodology dissertation |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

# fact-check-draft: Fact-check a draft

Checks a draft sentence by sentence against its sources and flags unsupported, overclaimed, wrong and outdated claims, then fixes them.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A finished draft (article, post, report, PRD, copy) plus its sources |
| **You get back** | Claim-by-claim verdicts with source locations, suggested fixes, and a corrected draft |
| **Search terms** | fact-check, verify claims, AI hallucination check, citation check, editorial review |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

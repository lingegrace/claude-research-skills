# active-recall: Flashcards and self-quiz from your notes

Turns your notes, slides or a chapter into practice questions, flashcards and a graded self-quiz with a review schedule.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Lecture notes, slides, a textbook chapter, a syllabus section |
| **You get back** | Question sets across Bloom's levels, flashcards with sources, a graded quiz session, and a Leitner review schedule |
| **Method** | Retrieval practice, spaced repetition (Leitner system), Bloom's taxonomy |
| **Search terms** | make flashcards from notes, practice questions generator, active recall study, spaced repetition schedule, 复习自测 |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

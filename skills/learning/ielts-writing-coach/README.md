# ielts-writing-coach: IELTS writing feedback and band estimate

Turns your IELTS Task 1 or Task 2 answer into a per-criterion band estimate with evidence, top fixes, an improved version and practice.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Your IELTS Writing Task 1 or Task 2 response, the question, and your target band |
| **You get back** | Estimated band per criterion with quotes from your essay, task coverage check, top 3 fixes, an improved version, and targeted practice |
| **Method** | Public IELTS Writing band descriptors (estimate, not an official score) |
| **Search terms** | IELTS writing feedback, IELTS essay band score, IELTS Task 2 correction, IELTS Task 1 report check, 雅思写作批改 |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

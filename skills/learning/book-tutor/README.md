# book-tutor: Chapter-by-chapter book study guide

Turns a book, chapters or your notes into a chapter-by-chapter study guide you work through actively.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Book chapters, excerpts or your reading notes, and your reading goal |
| **You get back** | Per chapter: key ideas, connections, check and discussion questions, application prompts, a one-page summary, and a review queue |
| **Method** | Active reading with SQ3R (Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review) |
| **Search terms** | book study guide, chapter summary, SQ3R reading method, book discussion questions, 读书笔记 |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

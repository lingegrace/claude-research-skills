# interview-practice: Mock interview with STAR feedback

Turns a job description and your CV into a realistic mock interview with feedback on every answer.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Target role and company, the job description, your CV, and the interview type (behavioural, case, PM, group interview) |
| **You get back** | A competency map, a mock interview one question at a time, feedback per answer, model answer outlines from your real experience, and a prep list |
| **Method** | STAR method, competency-based interviewing |
| **Search terms** | mock interview practice, behavioural interview questions, STAR method answers, case interview practice, 面试模拟 群面 |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

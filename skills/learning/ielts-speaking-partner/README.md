# ielts-speaking-partner: IELTS speaking mock test with feedback

Turns your target band and topics into a three-part mock IELTS Speaking test with criterion-by-criterion feedback.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Your target band, exam date and topic areas; your spoken (transcribed) or typed answers |
| **You get back** | A Part 1, 2 and 3 mock test with a cue card, estimated bands per criterion with evidence, useful language, a model answer outline, and next practice |
| **Method** | Public IELTS Speaking band descriptors (estimate, not an official score) |
| **Search terms** | IELTS speaking practice, IELTS speaking mock test, IELTS cue card Part 2, IELTS speaking band score, 雅思口语模考 |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

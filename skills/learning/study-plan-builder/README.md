# study-plan-builder: Exam revision plan and study timetable

Turns your exam date, syllabus, materials and weekly hours into a week-by-week revision plan with spaced reviews and mock checkpoints.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Exam or goal, exam date, syllabus and materials, hours available per week, current level |
| **You get back** | A knowledge framework of the syllabus, a week-by-week plan with daily tasks, spaced review slots, mock-test checkpoints, and a progress tracker |
| **Method** | Backward planning, spaced repetition, interleaving |
| **Search terms** | revision timetable, exam study plan, study schedule generator, how to plan revision, 备考计划 复习计划 |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

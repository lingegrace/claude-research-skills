# knowledge-gap-detector: Find your weak spots before the exam

Turns a topic or syllabus into a diagnostic quiz and a gap map that tells you what to study next, in order.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A topic, syllabus or exam spec, and your sense of what you've already studied |
| **You get back** | Sub-topic and prerequisite map, diagnostic results with evidence, a solid / shaky / missing gap map, and an ordered study list |
| **Method** | Diagnostic assessment, prerequisite mapping |
| **Search terms** | find knowledge gaps, diagnostic quiz, what should I study first, weak topics exam, 查漏补缺 |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

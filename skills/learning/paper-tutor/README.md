# paper-tutor: Understand an academic paper, step by step

Turns a paper you can't get through into a guided reading at your level, ending in referenced reading notes.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | An academic paper (PDF or text) and your background in the field |
| **You get back** | Argument map, section-by-section explanations, key terms, check questions, and reading notes (claim, method, findings, limits) with section or page references |
| **Method** | Three-pass reading approach (Keshav), retrieval practice checks |
| **Search terms** | how to read an academic paper, explain this paper, research paper summary, reading notes template, 读文献方法 |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

# customer-review-mining: Voice of customer from reviews

Mines G2, App Store, Reddit, NPS and support feedback into ranked pain points, outcomes and objections, in customers' own words.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Review exports or pasted reviews, NPS comments, support tickets, survey open-ends |
| **You get back** | Ranked pain points with counts and quotes, outcomes, objections, alternatives named, and what it means for roadmap and messaging |
| **Search terms** | voice of customer, review analysis, G2 reviews, customer feedback analysis, pain points |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

- Skill instructions: [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)

Install the whole collection in Claude Code with `/plugin marketplace add lingegrace/claude-research-skills`, or upload [customer-review-mining.zip](../../../dist/customer-review-mining.zip) in Claude.ai under Settings → Capabilities → Skills. See the [main README](../../../README.md).

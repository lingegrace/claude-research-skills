# competitor-messaging: Competitor messaging analysis and white space

Turns competitor homepages, landing pages and ads plus your own copy into a messaging map, the white space, and 3 angles that set you apart.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Competitor homepages, landing pages or ads (URLs or pasted copy) and your own homepage or positioning |
| **You get back** | Claims by company with exact quotes and sources, a messaging matrix, overlap and white space, and 3 differentiated messaging angles |
| **Method** | Claim extraction, messaging matrix, white-space analysis |
| **Search terms** | competitor messaging analysis, messaging comparison, competitor homepage analysis, differentiation, messaging white space |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

# landing-page-writer: Landing page copy that converts

Turns your product info, audience and offer into full landing page copy, section by section, with headline variants to test.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Product info or brief, who the page is for, the offer, and any value proposition, positioning, reviews or case studies |
| **You get back** | Hero headline, subhead and CTA, problem, solution, benefits, proof, objections/FAQ, final CTA, and 3 headline variants with test hypotheses |
| **Method** | Message hierarchy, PAS (Problem–Agitate–Solve), objection handling |
| **Search terms** | landing page copy, landing page copywriting, sales page template, hero headline, PAS copywriting formula |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

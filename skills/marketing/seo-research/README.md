# seo-research: SEO keyword research and keyword map

Turns a product or topic, its audience and your keyword exports into a keyword map with intent, funnel stage, priority and a page for every cluster.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Your product or topic, the audience and market, and optionally Ahrefs, Semrush or Google Search Console exports and your existing URLs |
| **You get back** | Keyword clusters tagged by search intent and funnel stage (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU), priorities with reasons, a page plan and cannibalisation risks |
| **Method** | Search-intent classification, topic clustering, funnel mapping |
| **Search terms** | keyword research, SEO keyword map, keyword clustering, search intent, SEO content plan |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

# decision-memo: Decision memo with options and recommendation

Turns research, analysis, notes or a team debate into an answer-first decision memo with options, risks and a recommendation.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Research, analysis, meeting notes, a Slack debate, data on a decision |
| **You get back** | A decision memo: context, the decision needed, options with pros/cons/risks, recommendation, what would change it, next steps and an evidence log |
| **Method** | SCQA framing (Minto Pyramid) + options analysis |
| **Search terms** | decision memo template, recommendation memo, options paper, research to memo, PM decision document |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

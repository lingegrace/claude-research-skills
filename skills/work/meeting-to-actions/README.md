# meeting-to-actions: Meeting notes to action items

Turns a meeting transcript, notes or chat log into decisions, owned action items, open questions and risks.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Meeting transcript, recording notes, Teams/Slack/Zoom chat log, your own scribbled notes |
| **You get back** | A 5-minute summary: decisions, action items (owner, due date, done-definition), open questions, risks, and optionally a decision memo |
| **Method** | Decision log + RACI-style ownership |
| **Search terms** | meeting minutes template, meeting notes to action items, meeting summary, action item list, follow-up email after meeting |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

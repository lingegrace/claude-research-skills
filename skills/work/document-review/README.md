# document-review: Review a draft document before sending

Reviews a proposal, report, plan, policy or deck text against its purpose and audience, with comments sorted by priority.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A work document (proposal, report, plan, policy, deck text) plus what it's for and who reads it |
| **You get back** | Prioritised comments (blocking / important / nice-to-have) on purpose fit, structure, evidence, clarity, risks and missing pieces, a claims check and a suggested revised outline |
| **Method** | Purpose, audience, structure and evidence review checklist |
| **Search terms** | document review checklist, feedback on a proposal, review my report, peer review a document, proofread business document |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

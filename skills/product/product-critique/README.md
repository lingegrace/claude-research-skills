# product-critique: Product teardown and UX heuristic review

Critiques a product, feature, prototype or screenshots against the user's job, Nielsen's 10 heuristics and severity.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A product, feature, prototype, screen recording or screenshots, plus the target user and goal |
| **You get back** | A structured critique: JTBD fit, usability issues by Nielsen's 10 heuristics, value clarity, friction points ranked by severity, and the top 3 concrete fixes |
| **Method** | Jobs-to-be-Done fit + Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics + severity rating |
| **Search terms** | product teardown, heuristic evaluation, Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics, UX review, product critique |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

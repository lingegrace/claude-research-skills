# ai-product-audit: AI product trust & risk audit

Audits an AI feature for transparency, dark patterns, overreliance, privacy, safety and compliance risk, with evidence and a fix for each issue.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Screenshots, user flows, demo access, spec, system prompt or policies |
| **You get back** | Ranked risk table with evidence and fixes, area-by-area ratings, quick wins, and items for legal review |
| **Search terms** | responsible AI, AI UX review, EU AI Act, AI risk assessment, trust and safety |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

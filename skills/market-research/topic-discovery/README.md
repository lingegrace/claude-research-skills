# topic-discovery: Content ideas and research topics, ranked

Turns a niche, an audience and the sources you follow into a ranked list of topics worth writing or studying next.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Your niche or field, your audience, and any newsletters, reports, competitor content, comments or search data you have |
| **You get back** | 10–20 ranked topics, each with the audience question, evidence of demand or gap, angle and difficulty, plus a question bank |
| **Method** | Question mining, content gap analysis, scoring by demand × authority × originality |
| **Search terms** | content ideas, blog topic ideas, content gap analysis, research topic ideas, what to write about |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

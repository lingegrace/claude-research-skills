# trend-research: Trend analysis and horizon scanning report

Turns a domain, a time horizon and the signals you've collected into a trend report separating signals, trends and drivers, with a watch-list.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A domain, a time horizon, and any news, reports, data or observations you have |
| **You get back** | Trend report with signals, trends and drivers, each with evidence, maturity, Three Horizons placement and implications, plus a watch-list of tripwires and a signal log |
| **Method** | STEEP horizon scanning, Three Horizons framework |
| **Search terms** | trend analysis, trend report, horizon scanning, STEEP analysis, Three Horizons framework |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

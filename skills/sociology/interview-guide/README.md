# interview-guide: Qualitative interview guide

Designs a semi-structured interview guide with themed open questions, probes, consent points and a bias check.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A research question or brief, participant details, interview length |
| **You get back** | An interview guide with consent script, warm-up, themed questions and probes, closing, interviewer notes, and bias-check changes |
| **Search terms** | interview guide, interview protocol, semi-structured interview, qualitative research, user interview |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

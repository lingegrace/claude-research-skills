# user-persona-builder: Personas & Jobs-to-be-Done

Builds evidence-based personas and JTBD statements from interviews, surveys, reviews and CRM notes, with every trait traced to evidence.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | Interview notes, survey data, reviews, CRM or sales notes, support tickets |
| **You get back** | 2–4 persona cards with JTBD, pains, triggers and buying criteria, a comparison table, and what to validate next |
| **Search terms** | user persona, jobs to be done, JTBD, ideal customer profile, ICP |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

- Skill instructions: [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)

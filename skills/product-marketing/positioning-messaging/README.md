# positioning-messaging: Positioning & messaging framework

Writes a positioning statement and messaging house from product docs, personas and competitor research, then drafts taglines and pitches.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | PRD, FAB analysis, personas, competitor research, win/loss notes, customer quotes |
| **You get back** | Positioning canvas and statement, messaging house with proof points, taglines, homepage heroes and pitches |
| **Search terms** | product positioning, messaging framework, messaging house, value proposition, April Dunford |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

- Skill instructions: [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)

# quantitative-analysis: Survey & quantitative analysis

Plans, runs and writes up survey or social science statistics, with assumption checks and interpretation that doesn't overclaim causation.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A dataset (CSV, Excel, SPSS, Stata) plus a research question |
| **You get back** | Cleaning log, descriptives, model results with CIs, assumption checks, plain-language interpretation, limitations and code |
| **Search terms** | survey analysis, regression, statistical analysis, SPSS, quantitative research |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

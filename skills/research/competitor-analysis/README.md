# competitor-analysis: Competitor analysis & sales battlecards

Builds competitor profiles, sales battlecards, market maps and pricing teardowns, with every source graded by how much to trust it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A business question about competitors (pricing, positioning, win-loss) |
| **You get back** | A competitor profile or battlecard with tiered sources, a mirror battlecard, FACT/READ/BET findings and ACT/WATCH/RESEARCH-MORE implications |
| **Method** | Source tiering T1–T5, signal clusters and a mirror-battlecard bias check |
| **Search terms** | competitor analysis, sales battlecard, competitive intelligence, pricing teardown, win-loss analysis |


A Claude Skill for **B2B competitive intelligence that doesn't just repeat vendor marketing**. It grades every source by tier (T1 SEC filings and pricing pages → T5 vendor marketing), counts independent signals rather than URLs, runs a **mirror battlecard** (your competitor's view of you) to catch sponsor bias, and grades findings **FACT / READ / BET** and implications **ACT / WATCH / RESEARCH-MORE**.

**Good for:** product marketing (PMM), product, sales enablement and strategy teams. Use it for competitor profiles, sales battlecards, market landscape maps, SaaS pricing and packaging teardowns, feature-parity analysis, and win-loss analysis.

**Modes:** `SCOUT` · `PROFILE` (default) · `BATTLECARD` · `LANDSCAPE` · `WIN-LOSS`

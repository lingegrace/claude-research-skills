# deep-research: Cited research report on any question

Turns a hard question into a cited research report with a claim–evidence table, confidence levels, counterevidence and open questions.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A business, policy or general question, plus any starting sources you already have |
| **You get back** | A research report with a short answer up front, findings per sub-question, a claim–evidence table, confidence ratings, counterevidence, open questions, a source list and what couldn't be accessed |
| **Method** | Question decomposition, source triangulation across independent source types, separate source identity and claim-support checks |
| **Search terms** | deep research report, desk research, research with citations, source triangulation, research briefing |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

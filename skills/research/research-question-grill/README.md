# research-question-grill: Research question interview

Interviews you one question at a time until a vague topic becomes a focused, answerable research question with scope, concepts and a falsifier.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A topic or rough idea |
| **You get back** | A research question brief: main and sub-questions, scope, concepts, falsifier, method fit, ready for sociology-deep-research |
| **Search terms** | research question, thesis topic, dissertation, research design, literature review |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

- Skill instructions: [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)

Install the whole collection in Claude Code with `/plugin marketplace add lingegrace/claude-research-skills`, or upload [research-question-grill.zip](../../../dist/research-question-grill.zip) in Claude.ai under Settings → Capabilities → Skills. See the [main README](../../../README.md).

# market-research: Market sizing and market analysis brief

Turns a market or product category and a business decision into a market brief with TAM/SAM/SOM sizing, drivers, structure and implications.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A market or product category, the decision you face (enter, price, which segment), and any reports or data you have |
| **You get back** | Market definition and segments, top-down and bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM with every assumption shown, PESTEL drivers, Five Forces, key players, implications and what to validate next |
| **Method** | TAM/SAM/SOM (top-down and bottom-up), PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces |
| **Search terms** | market research, market sizing, TAM SAM SOM, market analysis template, Porter's Five Forces analysis |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.

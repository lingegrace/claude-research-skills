# survey-design: Survey and questionnaire design

Turns a research or business question into a ready-to-field questionnaire with balanced scales, skip logic, a pilot checklist and an analysis plan.

| | |
|---|---|
| **You give it** | A research or business question, who you want to survey, and your constraints (length, channel) |
| **You get back** | A questionnaire with sections, exact wording, response scales and skip logic, a list of bias fixes, a pilot checklist and an analysis plan mapping every question to the research question |
| **Method** | Questionnaire design principles (one idea per item, no leading, loaded or double-barreled questions, balanced Likert scales, acquiescence bias checks) |
| **Search terms** | survey design, questionnaire design, how to write survey questions, Likert scale questions, survey template |

It always starts by asking what you're trying to achieve and what you already have, then works through fixed steps. Every claim is traced to your material or labeled as an assumption.
